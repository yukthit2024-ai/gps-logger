# User Prompt
when the mobile phone is tilted upside down, the app has to also turn aound and become vertical again, like google maps does

# Goals
Change the app's activity orientations from locked normal portrait to `fullSensor`. This allows the app to rotate to all 4 orientations (normal portrait, reverse portrait, landscape, and reverse landscape) based on the device sensor, matching Google Maps' behavior and working reliably on devices that block `sensorPortrait`.
